============================================================
              DittoCard · Quick Start Guide
============================================================

1. INSTALLATION:
   Drag and drop the "DittoCard" icon into the "Applications" folder.

2. FIRST LAUNCH (macOS Gatekeeper):
   Since DittoCard is built locally, macOS might show a standard security prompt on first launch.
   
   Method 1 (Recommended):
   Right-click (Control-click) on DittoCard in Applications -> click "Open" -> click "Open".
   
   Method 2 (via Terminal):
   sudo xattr -cr /Applications/DittoCard.app

3. ZERO PREREQUISITES:
   DittoCard is self-contained for its build architecture on Apple Silicon or Intel Macs.
   No Homebrew, Python packages, or external tools required!

4. HOW TO USE:
   [Apple Wallet Cards]
   - Connect your iPhone via USB cable and tap "Trust this Computer".
   - Open DittoCard (the device status badge will turn green).
   - Click "Scan Cards".
   - On your iPhone, double-click the Side button, complete any unlock prompt, and tap your card.
   - Choose a custom skin image for a card, or drag & drop one onto it. The card flashes automatically.
   - Force-close the Wallet app on your iPhone to see your new designs.
